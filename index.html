About Me

I am a Full Stack Developer with expertise in both front-end and back-end development, skilled in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and React Router. My approach goes beyond writing code. I focus on deeply understanding problems from the user’s perspective, putting myself in their shoes to design solutions that are intuitive, efficient, and reliable.

On the front end, I create clean, responsive interfaces that prioritize usability and accessibility. On the back end, I ensure seamless integration and performance, building systems that work smoothly behind the scenes. Alongside my professional work, I also have a good knowledge of Python, using it to experiment, automate, and expand my problem-solving toolkit.

With a balance of technical depth and user-centered thinking, I aim to deliver solutions that not only function well but also feel natural and meaningful to the people who use them.
